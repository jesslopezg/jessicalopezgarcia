01 / ABOUT

Research informed by organizations and the people who shape them.

I am a professor and researcher at the School of Digital Transformation, Universidad Tecnológica de Bolívar, in Cartagena, Colombia. My academic work examines the adoption and organizational integration of artificial intelligence, its relationship with workforce change, and the capabilities required to navigate digital transformation.

I earned a PhD in Administrative Sciences from Universidad Autónoma de Baja California (2026). My work connects management research, emerging technologies, and applied learning, drawing on collaborations across Mexico, Colombia, and international academic networks.

04 / TEACHING

Connecting research with applied learning.

My teaching focuses on the application of artificial intelligence and research methods in business and organizational contexts. At Universidad Tecnológica de Bolívar, I teach courses including Applications of Artificial Intelligence and Market Research.

Classroom projects emphasize evidence-based decision-making, hands-on experimentation, and ethical consideration of AI tools and data.

05 / ACADEMIC SERVICE

Collaboration beyond the classroom.

I contribute to international academic and professional communities through IEEE Technology and Engineering Management Society (TEMS) activities, educational webinars, and conference communications and organization.

These activities support knowledge exchange among researchers, educators, and practitioners working at the intersection of technology and management.
